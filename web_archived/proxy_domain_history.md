# Proxy domain archive history

CDX is ordered by URL, so every page of the first-capture-per-URL index was scanned before taking the minimum timestamp. Domains with no pre-2020 capture were checked across all available years.

Checked 71 domains; 71 complete; 9 have earliest captures during 2017–2019. Earliest captures before 2017 do not count toward this shortlist; the unfiltered history is retained below.
